Registration & grades Registrar • Solved

Grade shows "I" (incomplete) after the final exam

41 students fixed this themselves
  1. Check the grade sheet notes in the portal. An "I" means the instructor has not submitted a final mark.
  2. Email your course instructor and copy the course coordinator within 5 working days.
  3. If the mark is not updated after 7 days, open a complaint under Registration & grades with the course code.
Answer by Eng. Mona Fathy (Registrar)
Study plan & courses Academic Advising • Solved

How to drop a course after the add/drop deadline

67 students fixed this themselves
  1. Late drops need advisor approval and a valid documentary reason.
  2. Open a complaint under Study plan & courses and attach supporting documents.
  3. Your advisor reviews and forwards the request to the Dean. You receive a decision in 3 to 5 working days.
Answer by Dr. Salma Nabil (Academic Advising)
Portal, Wi-Fi & accounts IT Services • Solved

Cannot log in to the portal after a password reset

122 students fixed this themselves
  1. Wait 15 minutes after the reset for directory synchronization.
  2. Sign in with your student ID number, not your personal email address.
  3. Clear browser cookies and try an incognito / private window.
  4. Still blocked? Open an IT complaint with a screenshot of the error.
Answer by Ms. Nour Gamal (IT Services)
Fees & payments Finance Office • Solved

Installment shows unpaid after a bank transfer

58 students fixed this themselves
  1. Keep the official bank deposit receipt with the transaction reference number.
  2. Wait 24 hours for the automated bank reconciliation sync.
  3. If it still shows unpaid, open a Fees & payments complaint and attach the receipt image.
Answer by Mr. Adel Ragab (Finance Office)
Portal, Wi-Fi & accounts IT Services • Solved

Wi-Fi asks for login every few minutes across campus

94 students fixed this themselves
  1. Forget the "SUT-Student" Wi-Fi network and reconnect.
  2. Turn off "Random MAC address" (Private Address) for this network in your phone or laptop settings.
  3. If disconnections continue in specific halls, open an IT complaint with your room number.
Answer by Mr. Hesham Adly (IT Services)
Registration & grades Registrar • Solved

Official transcript: how long and how much

83 students fixed this themselves
  1. Request the official transcript in the portal under Documents.
  2. Pay the administrative fee at the Finance Office or via online portal.
  3. Pickup takes 3 working days from Student Affairs. Sealed copies for external universities take 5 working days.
Answer by Mr. Karim Saad (Registrar)
Study plan & courses Academic Advising • Solved

A prerequisite blocks me from registering for an advanced course

39 students fixed this themselves
  1. Check your study plan matrix to confirm which prerequisite is missing.
  2. If you passed an equivalent transfer course, ask your advisor for a prerequisite waiver form.
  3. The waiver is applied directly in your registration portal upon approval.
Answer by Dr. Tarek Younes (Academic Advising)
Labs, library & facilities Facilities • Solved

Lab access card does not open the electronic lab doors

27 students fixed this themselves
  1. Hold the card steadily against the reader for two full seconds.
  2. Ask the lab technician to check your name on the authorized enrolled roster.
  3. If missing from the list, open a Facilities complaint with your course and lab code.
Answer by Ms. Reem Salah (Facilities)
