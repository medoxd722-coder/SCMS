Everything right now is messy, slow, and unorganized.

01

Huge lines at the Registrar

Students stand in busy lines for hours just to find out the Registrar desk cannot even help them with their issue.

02

Nobody knows who to ask

Students bounce from office to office between advisors, departments, and IT with zero clear direction.

03

Same questions every day

Staff waste hours answering the exact same complaints over and over because solved answers are not saved anywhere.

04

Big issues get ignored

When 500 students face the same problem, the university does not see how big it is until everyone complains in person.

Log in once. Then pick the path that fits your problem.

Students sign in with their university ID. Every route ends in an answer that is saved for the next student.

Public forum

Post general campus issues and upvote the ones that affect you. The most upvoted problems rise to the top for the university to fix first.

Make a complaint

For personal issues like grades or registration. Your complaint goes straight to the right advisor, not the wrong desk, with live status.

Solved archive

Resolved complaints are saved in a searchable archive. Students can find verified step-by-step answers to common problems.

Why this beats what students use today.

Other options Why they fail What we offer instead
Registrar desk and emails Long physical lines, lost emails, and nobody knows who handles what. Instant routing to the right advisor plus live complaint status.
WhatsApp and Facebook groups Messy, unofficial, full of rumors, and university staff are not there. An official university forum where upvotes bring real staff replies.
Big corporate tools (Zendesk) Costs thousands of dollars a month and is not built for students. Built specifically for SUT students & staff at zero per-user license cost.

How this becomes a real success, not a side project.

STEP 01 · Real adoption

Plugged into the uni website

It lives directly on the official SUT university website using student ID login.

STEP 02 · Less work for staff

Empty the Registrar lines

The solved archive stops students from asking the same questions 100 times.

STEP 03 · Smart rollout

Start in one faculty, then expand

Test with real advisors in one department, prove it cuts lines, then open to the whole university.

STEP 04 · Management power

Accountability at the top

Management sees which problems have the most upvotes and which departments are slow.

Fewer lines at the Registrar. Faster help from advisors. A memory of every solved problem.
